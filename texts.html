Texts

A collection of philosophical works, organized by period and accompanied by explanations of their arguments, concepts, questions, and intellectual significance.

Ancient Philosophy

Republic

Plato

Justice, the ideal state, education, knowledge, and the philosopher's role in society.

Apology

Plato

Socrates' defense of his life, philosophy, and commitment to examining human knowledge.

Nicomachean Ethics

Aristotle

Virtue, happiness, character, reason, and the good life.

Early Modern Philosophy

Meditations on First Philosophy

René Descartes

Doubt, certainty, consciousness, God, and the foundations of knowledge.

19th Century Philosophy

Beyond Good and Evil

Friedrich Nietzsche

Morality, truth, power, perspectivism, and the criticism of traditional philosophy.

Fear and Trembling

Søren Kierkegaard

Faith, ethics, individuality, and the story of Abraham and Isaac.

Philosophy in the Bedroom

Marquis de Sade

Morality, liberty, nature, desire, and radical challenges to conventional ethics.

20th Century Philosophy

On Sense and Reference

Gottlob Frege

The distinction between sense and reference and its consequences for language and meaning.

On Denoting

Bertrand Russell

Definite descriptions, logical form, existence, and Russell's theory of descriptions.

On Referring

P. F. Strawson

A response to Russell concerning reference, descriptions, and ordinary language.

The Myth of Sisyphus

Albert Camus

The absurd, suicide, meaning, and the problem of living in a world without ultimate meaning.
